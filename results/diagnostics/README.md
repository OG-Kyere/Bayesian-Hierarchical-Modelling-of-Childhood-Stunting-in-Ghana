# Interim diagnostic results

These files are **not final thesis inference**. They are independent clustering diagnostics fitted with logistic generalized estimating equations (GEE) while the primary PyMC/NUTS environment is unavailable.

The exchangeable working correlations were:

- household: 0.154
- community: 0.024

These quantities are not interchangeable with Bayesian random-effect variances, ICCs, VPCs, or MORs. They are retained because they provide an independent check that within-household dependence is materially stronger than within-community dependence in the current analytic sample.

The fixed-effect directions were also broadly consistent with the completed community-level Bayesian pilot, which is reassuring but not used to replace the planned household/community Bayesian model.
