\chapter*{Research Transparency}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Research Transparency}
This report documents an independent secondary-data research project. It is not presented as a submission for a university degree. The repository distinguishes verified descriptive estimates, legacy exploratory Bayesian outputs, and new validation runs. Original DHS microdata and row-level posterior outputs are not redistributed. Final authorship and contributor statements should accurately describe the work performed and any computational or writing assistance.
