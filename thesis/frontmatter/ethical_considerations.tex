\chapter*{Ethical Considerations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Ethical Considerations}
\begin{singlespace}
This study is a secondary analysis of de-identified data from the 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey obtained through authorized access from The DHS Program. No new primary data were collected from human participants, and no attempt was made to identify respondents, households, or survey locations beyond the geographic information contained in the authorized recode files. The original GDHS was conducted under the ethical and informed-consent procedures described in the official survey documentation. Raw DHS microdata are not redistributed with this thesis or its public reproducibility repository.
