\chapter*{Ethical Considerations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Ethical Considerations}
\begin{singlespace}
The 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey received ethical clearance from the Ghana Health Service Ethical Review Committee and the ICF Institutional Review Board, and the original survey used informed-consent procedures under its approved protocol. This independent study is a secondary analysis of de-identified DHS data obtained through authorized access from The DHS Program. No primary data were collected, there was no direct participant contact, and no attempt was made to identify respondents, households, or survey locations beyond information available in the authorized recode files. Restricted DHS microdata are not redistributed with this report or its public reproducibility repository.
\end{singlespace}
