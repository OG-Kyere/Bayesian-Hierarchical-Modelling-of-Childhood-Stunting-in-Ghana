\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\begin{singlespace}
This independent research project examines associations with childhood stunting and residual household and survey-community heterogeneity using the 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey. The verified analytic sample comprises 4,928 de facto children aged 0--59 months with valid height-for-age measurements, nested within 3,545 households and 616 represented survey communities. Survey-weighted prevalence is 17.4\% (normal-approximation 95\% confidence interval: 15.8\%--18.9\%). Sequential Bayesian logistic models incorporate community and household random intercepts, water and sanitation source/facility types, and maternal education. The maternal-education extension and its comparator use the same 4,503 children. The repaired analysis workflow specifies prior-predictive checks, joint-posterior convergence requirements, survey-weight and missing-information sensitivities, and posterior-predictive assessment. Legacy short-run Bayesian estimates remain exploratory because hierarchical variance diagnostics were inadequate and parts of their provenance were incomplete. Accordingly, this working abstract reports no confirmed inferential effect estimates or household/community ranking. The final abstract and substantive conclusions will be generated only after the production analysis and scientific validation are complete.
\end{singlespace}
