\chapter{Supplementary Results and Output Catalogue}
\label{app:supplementary_results}

This appendix brings the project's main aggregate outputs into the compiled long-form report. The underlying CSV and SVG files remain in the \texttt{results/} directory so that the numerical outputs and publication figures can also be inspected directly. The appendix is descriptive and diagnostic; it does not introduce a new model or alter the locked results reported in Chapter~\ref{chap:results}.
