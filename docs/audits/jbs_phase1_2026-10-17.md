## High-priority issues
1. **Convergence and model identification**: All four archived fits have parameter R-hat above 1.01. Check every alpha/beta/tau/z_community/z_household and deterministic u_community/u_household, including worst individual-effect diagnostics, chains, rank plots, MCSE and prior-vs-posterior behaviour. Compare the household variance finding across household sizes; 2,398 of 3,545 households contribute one child. Do not simply suppress problematic random effects from diagnostic reporting.
2. **DHS source/facility classification**: src/05_wash_model.py and src/06_maternal_education_model.py use ~(value.astype(str).isin(IMPROVED_...)), so missing/unexpected categories can be silently classified as unimproved. Do not alter classifications on conjecture: first tabulate all observed hv201/hv205 labels, missingness and counts in the authorised dataset, then make an explicit whitelist with failure for unexpected codes or justified missing handling. Use equivalent checks across main and sensitivity scripts.
3. **Survey design**: Descriptive prevalence uses hv005/hv021/hv022; main hierarchical Bayesian models do not fully encode survey weights/strata as a probability sampling model. Weighted pseudo-posterior is a sensitivity check, not proof of full design correction. Explain estimand and limitations.
4. **Maternal education missingness**: 425 missing (8.624%). Descriptive weighted stunting among those cases is 21.09%; in main analysis the complete-case sample has 4,503. Missing-category model is supportive but not proof missingness is ignorable.
5. **Posterior source provenance**: docs/locked_model3_result_provenance.md explicitly says archived locked chain-level NetCDF is not version controlled, and public rerun does not exactly replay it. Access authorised local copies privately for definitive verification; never commit them.
6. **Predictive validation**: Pointwise PSIS-LOO leaves children out but retains estimated household/community hierarchy. For generalization to new households/communities use a correctly configured grouped validation if that claim is necessary; otherwise clearly limit claim.
