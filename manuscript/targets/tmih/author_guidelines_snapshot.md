# TMIH targeting snapshot — 25 September 2026

This file records the journal requirements used to retarget the submission package.

## Verified requirements

- Research Articles are accepted.
- Main-body word limit is 3,500 words in principle; exceptions may be allowed for studies that need additional space.
- Abstract maximum is 300 words and must be structured as **Objectives, Methods, Results, Conclusions**.
- Main text should follow IMRD.
- Statistical methods should be sufficiently detailed for verification by a knowledgeable reader with access to the data.
- Results should report absolute numbers as well as derived summaries where relevant.
- Tables and figures should be limited to those needed to support the paper and should not duplicate one another.
- Uncertainty intervals are encouraged rather than reliance on hypothesis testing alone.
- Vancouver is the publication reference style; Harvard or Vancouver can be used at submission.
- A data-accessibility statement with a repository link is encouraged.
- A word count and at least five suggested reviewers are requested.
- Author names, addresses, email addresses, affiliations, degrees, corresponding-author details, the author statement, and word count should be ready at submission.
- ORCID is encouraged.
- Authors must declare interests and funding.
- New submissions are currently routed through Wiley Authors; submissions begun before 30 July 2025 remain in Editorial Manager.
- No page charges are imposed. Immediate OnlineOpen is optional.
- Wiley requires transparent AI disclosure when GenAI contributes to manuscript development or research processes; code-development/analysis assistance belongs in the Methods-level disclosure.

## Note on a wording inconsistency

The current TMIH author page says submissions should include a word count and reviewer suggestions in the manuscript, but its auto-extraction tips also say not to include material such as word count that does not normally appear in the published article. The TMIH target therefore keeps the word count/reviewer suggestions in a clearly labelled **Editorial Information (for submission only)** section and duplicates them in the submission support files so they can be removed easily after editorial processing.
