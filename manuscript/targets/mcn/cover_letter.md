Dear Editors of Maternal & Child Nutrition,

Please consider our manuscript, “Household and Community Heterogeneity in Childhood Stunting in Ghana: A Bayesian Hierarchical Analysis of the 2022 Demographic and Health Survey,” for publication as a Research Article in Maternal & Child Nutrition.

Childhood stunting in Ghana has declined substantially, but national averages conceal marked household, socioeconomic, and geographic inequalities. The manuscript uses the nationally representative 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey to address a focused question that has received less attention in the most recent Ghanaian literature: how much residual heterogeneity in childhood stunting is concentrated at the household level relative to the survey-community level, and how stable is that hierarchy after WASH and maternal education are introduced?

The analysis includes 4,928 children nested within 3,545 households and 616 survey communities. Bayesian hierarchical logistic models show substantially greater residual household than community heterogeneity. The final model also identifies robust associations with child age, sex, household wealth, drinking-water source, and higher maternal education. The analysis is supported by prior, survey-weight, missing-data, age-functional-form, and WASH-coding sensitivity analyses, posterior predictive checks, and PSIS-LOO diagnostics.

We believe the manuscript fits the journal’s focus on child nutrition, socioeconomic and environmental influences on nutrition, nutritional risk assessment, and research methods. The paper is deliberately framed as an applied child-nutrition study rather than as a claim that Bayesian modelling of stunting in Ghana is itself novel.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors will approve the submitted version. The 2022 Ghana DHS microdata are restricted and cannot be redistributed by the authors; eligible researchers can obtain them directly from The DHS Program. Reproducibility code and non-identifying aggregate outputs are maintained separately.
