- Maximum main-text word count: 5,000 words excluding abstract and references.
- Abstract maximum: 250 words.
- Abstract is unstructured.
- Main structure: Abstract; Introduction; Key Messages; Methods; Results; Discussion; optional Conclusion; References; legends; tables/figures.
- Key Messages: 3–5 messages, approximately 80–100 words total.
- Main-paper figures/tables: maximum total of 5.
- Extra diagnostics/methods can be submitted as Supplementary Appendix.
- Double-blind peer review: the main manuscript must not identify authors.
- References: APA 7 style requested.
- Data accessibility statement expected.
- Generative-AI use must be disclosed at submission.
